How rebates work

A rebate is money that comes back

A pharmacy buys a drug at list price. If it buys enough, the maker returns part of the price at the end of the quarter.

  1. 01

    Buy

    The pharmacy orders from its wholesaler, as usual.

  2. 02

    Count

    Each quarter the maker counts what was bought.

  3. 03

    Pay back

    Above the target, a share of the spend is returned.
